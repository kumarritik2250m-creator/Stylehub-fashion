Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about your STARHUB shopping experience.

How can I track my order?
Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an automated tracking link via SMS and WhatsApp. You can also view real-time updates directly from your Account Orders section.
What is the return and exchange window?
We offer a hassle-free 7-day easy return and exchange policy from the date of delivery. Garments must be unwashed, unworn, and have their original price tags intact. Doorstep reverse pickup is 100% free across India. Learn more on our Returns & Refunds page.
How long will it take for my order to arrive?
Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST are dispatched on the same business day. Delivery takes 1-3 business days for major metropolitan areas and 4-7 business days for the rest of India via our premium logistics partners (Bluedart, Delhivery, Xpressbees).
What payment methods are supported?
We accept all major payment methods including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD). All prepaid transactions are encrypted and secured via PCI-DSS compliant gateways.
How will I receive my refund?
For prepaid orders, the refund is automatically credited back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days after item inspection. For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, our team will disburse the refund to your provided UPI ID or bank account within 3-5 business days.
Are there any shipping charges?
Prepaid orders enjoy 100% Free Shipping nationwide. For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, a nominal handling and verification fee of ₹49 applies at checkout.

Need to speak with our support desk?

Our dedicated support team is available Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST to assist you.
