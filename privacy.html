Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026. Your privacy and data security are our top priorities.

1. Introduction & Entity Details

This Privacy Policy outlines how Xpord Private Limited ("we", "us", "our"), operating the consumer clothing brand STARHUB, collects, protects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit or make a purchase from our web application.

We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality and integrity of your information in strict accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable consumer protection guidelines of India.

2. Information We Collect

To fulfill orders and provide high-quality support, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact & Identity Details: Full name, email address, mobile phone number, and delivery addresses.
  • Transaction Records: Products ordered, transaction identifiers, payment method choice (Prepaid/COD), and return/exchange records.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, device characteristics, IP address, and cookie tokens to preserve cart session state and enhance user interface responsiveness.

Note: We do not store credit/debit card numbers or net banking passwords on our servers. All monetary transactions are processed directly through 256-bit SSL encrypted, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate business and fulfillment purposes:

  • Processing, packing, and delivering your apparel orders to your shipping address.
  • Sending automated shipment notifications and live tracking links via SMS, WhatsApp, and Email.
  • Processing doorstep reverse pickups, exchanges, and financial refunds.
  • Preventing fraud, safeguarding accounts, and maintaining platform security.
  • Providing responsive customer care assistance via our support channels.

4. Sharing & Disclosure of Information

We do never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. We only share necessary data with trusted operational partners:

  • Logistics Partners: Delivery carriers (Bluedart, Delhivery, Xpressbees) to ensure timely doorstep transit.
  • Payment Gateways: RBI-authorized payment processors for secure transaction settlement.
  • Legal Authorities: Only when required by law, subpoena, or competent governmental authority.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard technical and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your personal data. All network communications are encrypted via HTTPS (Transport Layer Security).
