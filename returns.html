1. Returns & Exchange Eligibility

At STARHUB, we take pride in our premium fabric quality and tailored craftsmanship. If sizing is not ideal or you are not completely satisfied with your order, we offer seamless returns and exchanges under the following conditions:

  • The return/exchange request is initiated within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and undamaged with all original product tags intact.
  • Items must be enclosed in their original packaging material where feasible.

3. Refund Processing Timelines

Refunds are initiated immediately upon completion of item inspection:

  • Prepaid Orders (UPI / Card / NetBanking): Refund is credited back directly to the source payment method within 5 to 7 business days depending on your bank's settlement cycle.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Our support desk will contact you to collect your preferred UPI ID or Bank Account Details (Account Number & IFSC). Funds are credited via IMPS/NEFT transfer within 3 to 5 business days.

4. Damaged or Incorrect Item Received

In the rare event that an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please reach out to us within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos. We will arrange an immediate priority replacement at no extra charge.
